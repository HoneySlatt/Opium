# Full audit, 2026-10-31

The whole app, desktop and phone, examined before the project came under its method, and fixed the
same day. Recorded on 2026-11-05 from the owner's notes and checked against `git log`: the list of
findings itself was not kept, so each fix is known by its commit, not by the finding it answered.

## Scope

- **Code**: the whole workspace and the Android app, from `6ddbdda` (2026-10-31).
- **Dimensions**, one read-only reviewing agent each: the services; security and privacy; the
  desktop's performance; the desktop's bugs; the player and Opium Connect; the phone's playback and
  battery; the phone's interface.
- **Not covered**: the method's audit dimensions did not exist yet. Tests, readability,
  documentation, dependencies and accessibility were not examined as dimensions of their own.

## Findings

About 130, by the owner's notes. They were not written down one by one, and how each was verified
is not recorded.

## The owner's decisions

- Lyrics sites other than LRCLIB are off unless the user turns them on
  ([ADR-0008](../adr/0008-the-lyrics-sources.md)).
- The APK's release signing key comes later; the release build stays signed with the debug key
  (ROADMAP, Stage 2).

## Fixed

Nine commits, `1125297` to `e2ebc85`:

- `1125297` Phone, privacy: nothing goes into backups or transfers; covers reach other apps only
  under random keys; other apps control only what plays.
- `5abe880` Privacy and safety: unreadable settings lose only themselves; the desktop's files are
  the user's alone; pairing waits longer after each failed try; lyrics sites other than LRCLIB off.
- `e30ff02` Services: a refusal is no longer taken for a sign-out; a station out of reach says so.
- `ce7b9b6` Desktop: what failed to load is asked again; a change no service takes is undone and
  said.
- `47f7b1f` Phone, playback: refused addresses made anew, a lost network waited for.
- `295a789` Phone, screens: a page gone back to is as it was left.
- `20eb1f2` Desktop audio: a queued song changed is dropped cleanly; a seek the output cannot take
  no longer ends or hangs the track.
- `a1fa3b1` Phone: a library read within the hour shows as kept at start.
- `e2ebc85` Phone: rotation and the evening's dark mode keep the screen where it was.

Then a follow-up the same day, `4bf0551` to `dee0c97`: Sign Out on the desktop (`4bf0551`);
Navidrome covers kept unsigned, signed only as fetched (`5228297`); downloads resumed after a drop
(`fa7e546`); Android Auto song ids per list (`7c0662d`); the desktop's output fading out when its
ring runs dry (`dee0c97`).

## Left on purpose

The owner is asked before any is taken up. Open items are in the known debt of
[`../PLAN.md`](../PLAN.md#known-debt).

- SoundCloud's clear HLS is joined whole before it plays: the growing reader needs the length up
  front, and Symphonia's fragmented-MP4 reader was judged risky.
- No stream cache on the phone: Apple Music's and SoundCloud's addresses are signed per request, a
  live playlist must not be cached, and a wrong key would play the wrong song.
- The phone: Opium Connect's node built on the main thread.
- The phone's interface: the page tint recomposing for 450 ms, recompositions of the whole app,
  accessibility labels, touch targets.
- The desktop: Apple's own token headers kept on redirects.
- The player: a scratch buffer reallocated on the real-time thread, 32-bit sources through `f32`, a
  hang on closing while a read is stuck.
